# Mission Control — No-Slop Rules

Slop is output that looks like it was generated by a generic AI prompt with no taste, no business context, and no visual judgment.

Slop costs clients. A sloppy site does not get calls.

These rules are absolute. They apply at every tier.

---

## Visual Slop — Never Produce

### Generic AI Gradients
Do not use:
- Default purple → blue gradient
- Generic dark blue → teal gradient
- Oversaturated neon color schemes
- Random glow effects with no connection to the business

Use instead: industry gradients defined in the Visual Language Bible (doc 19).

### Generic Typography
Do not default to:
- Inter as the headline font (without specific justification)
- System font stacks as a "premium" choice
- Bold + italic on the same element for emphasis

Use instead: intentional font pairings per industry type (Visual Language Bible §16).

### Decorative 3D With No Purpose
Do not use:
- Floating cubes or geometric shapes as decoration
- Random orb or sphere 3D elements
- Abstract WebGL that has no connection to the business

Use instead: business-specific 3D per industry recipes (Visual Language Bible §17).

### Stock Photo Look
Do not use:
- Hand-shaking business people
- Generic smiling team photos from stock sites
- Photos clearly from a different industry
- Crowd scenes irrelevant to the business
- Fake corporate "diversity" stock imagery

Use instead: business-specific visuals, client-supplied photos, or AI-generated images using the image generation prompts from the Visual Language Bible (§18–§19).

### Random Icon Sets
Do not use:
- Icon grids with no clear purpose
- Generic checkmarks or stars as design elements
- Icons that are not specifically tied to services or trust points

Use instead: specific service icons tied to the business type.

## Copy Slop — Never Write

### Generic Headlines
Do not write:
- "Welcome to [Company Name]"
- "Your trusted partner for [industry]"
- "We are committed to excellence"
- "Serving the [city] area"
- "Quality you can trust"
- "Solutions for your business"
- "Taking care of all your [needs]"
- "One-stop shop for..."

Use instead: outcome-focused, specific, trust-building headlines per industry.

### Vague CTAs
Do not use:
- "Learn More"
- "Click Here"
- "Submit"
- "Find Out More"
- "Discover"

Use instead: specific action CTAs: "Get a Free Estimate", "Schedule Free Inspection", "Request Service Today", "Call Now", "Get Pricing".

### Fake Statistics
Do not invent:
- "10,000+ satisfied customers"
- "100% satisfaction guaranteed" (without source)
- "#1 Rated in [city]" (without source)
- "25+ years of experience" (unless confirmed from client's site)

Use instead: real data from the scraped site, or clearly formatted placeholders: "[INSERT YEARS] years in business".

### Corporate Filler
Do not write:
- "At [Company Name], we believe..."
- "Our mission is to..."
- "We strive to..."
- "Our team of dedicated professionals..."
- "State-of-the-art solutions..."

Use instead: direct claims, proof, and outcomes.

## Layout Slop — Never Produce

### Generic Three-Card Section
Do not produce the default layout:
- Three equal white cards
- Generic icon + title + body copy
- No visual differentiation
- No hierarchy

Use instead: elevated service card layout with industry-specific visuals, trust signals, and hierarchy.

### Hidden CTAs
Do not produce any layout where:
- The primary CTA is below the fold on mobile
- The phone number requires scrolling to find
- The CTA button is smaller than the surrounding text

### Cluttered Hero
Do not produce hero sections with:
- More than one primary CTA (one primary + one secondary maximum)
- Three or more competing animations
- Blocks of body text in the hero
- Navigation-style lists as hero content

### Excessive Stagger
Do not produce:
- More than 5 staggered scroll-reveal elements per section
- Stagger delay exceeding 100ms per element
- Full-page cascading animations that delay reading

## Slop QA Check

Before any direction or build-spec is confirmed, run this mental check:

1. Could this design have been produced by any generic AI website builder? If yes — it is slop. Redesign.
2. Does any copy sound like it was written for no specific business? If yes — it is slop. Rewrite.
3. Is every visual element tied to this specific industry and business? If not — it is slop. Replace.
4. Can the visitor understand the offer in 5 seconds? If not — it is slop. Simplify.
5. Is the CTA reachable on mobile without scrolling? If not — it fails. Fix.

---

## The Standard

Mission Control does not ship slop.

If a build would embarrass us to show to a client, it does not ship.
If a build looks like a free website builder template, it does not ship.
If a build fails the slop check, it returns to the design stage.
