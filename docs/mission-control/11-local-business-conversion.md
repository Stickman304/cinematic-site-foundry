# Mission Control — Local Business Conversion Rules

Local service businesses have specific conversion patterns that differ from SaaS, e-commerce, and media sites.

Every Mission Control build must apply these rules.

---

## The Local Business Visitor

A visitor to a local service business website is typically:

- Already looking for this service (high intent)
- Comparing 2–4 competitors at the same time
- On mobile, likely while at work or at home with a problem
- Making a decision in minutes, not days
- Trusting signals: local presence, reviews, licenses, years in business

The job is not to explain the service in depth.
The job is to give them one reason to call or request a quote before they click the back button.

---

## The Five Conversion Signals

Every local business site must communicate all five:

1. **What they do** — Immediately clear. No guessing.
2. **Where they serve** — City, county, or service area visible near the top.
3. **Why trust them** — At least one trust proof: years, license, reviews, certifications.
4. **What to do** — One clear CTA: call, request quote, or schedule.
5. **How to reach them** — Phone number visible at all times.

If any of these are missing, the site fails conversion basics regardless of visual quality.

---

## Mobile CTA Rules

On mobile (320px–430px):

- Sticky bottom bar with phone number and "Call Now" button
- Hero must have a tap-to-call button visible without scrolling
- Quote form must be reachable in one scroll or one tap
- Do not require 4+ taps to find contact information

Mobile CTA implementation:
```
Position: fixed bottom
Height: 56–64px
Content: [Phone Icon] (216) 555-0100 | [GET A QUOTE]
Background: brand CTA color
Text: high contrast
z-index: above all content
```

---

## Trust Architecture

Trust elements must be placed strategically:

**Above fold (in hero):**
- Years in business or license status
- Star rating (if 4.5+)
- City/area served

**Below hero:**
- Trust strip: 4–6 icons with short labels (Licensed & Insured, Free Estimates, 5-Star Rated, etc.)
- Review section: real reviews (not fabricated)

**Near the quote form:**
- Reassurance copy: "No obligation. Free estimate. Same-day response."
- Privacy line: "We never share your information."

**In footer:**
- Full license number if public
- BBB or association membership
- Physical address if applicable

---

## Quote Form Rules

The quote form is the highest-value element on the page.

Form requirements:
- 3–5 fields maximum (name, phone, service type, message)
- No email required if phone is collected
- Submit button must be CTA color, full width, large
- Confirmation: immediate "We'll call you within [timeframe]" message
- Never call the button "Submit"

Good button labels:
- Get My Free Quote
- Request My Free Estimate
- Schedule Free Inspection
- Get Pricing

---

## Review Block Rules

Reviews are the strongest trust signal for local businesses.

Display rules:
- Show at least 3 reviews
- Source attribution (Google, Facebook, Houzz)
- Star visual (5-star display)
- First name + last initial
- Date (within last 2 years)
- Relevant to the specific service if possible

Do not:
- Fabricate reviews
- Use generic "Happy Customer" attribution
- Show reviews under 4 stars unless there is a specific response strategy

---

## Service Area Section

Local SEO and trust both require a visible service area.

Options:
- Text list: "Serving Cleveland, Parma, Strongsville, Middleburg Heights, Berea, and surrounding areas"
- Map embed (Google Maps or static image)
- City grid with named locations

The service area section must be present on every local business site.

---

## Emergency/Urgency CTA Pattern

For services with emergency use cases (HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, towing, restoration):

Add an urgency band or floating element:
- "24/7 Emergency Service Available — Call Now"
- Background: CTA accent color
- Placement: below hero or sticky alongside mobile CTA

Emergency CTA must be a phone link on mobile (not a form).

---

## Conversion Section Order

The highest-converting section order for local service businesses:

1. Hero (CTA + trust proof)
2. Trust strip
3. Services (cards with brief descriptions and CTAs)
4. Social proof (reviews + stats)
5. Why us / differentiators
6. Project gallery or before/after (if applicable)
7. Service area
8. Final CTA / quote form
9. Footer with phone + license + address

Do not bury the quote form at the bottom with no intermediate CTA.

## Industry-Specific Conversion Notes

| Industry | Primary Action | Secondary Action | Urgency Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Roofing | Free inspection | View gallery | High (storm season) |
| HVAC | Schedule service | Get pricing | Very high (emergency breakdown) |
| Plumbing | Call now | Schedule | Extreme (active leak) |
| Electrician | Call now | Schedule | Very high (safety) |
| Concrete | Get estimate | View projects | Low-medium |
| Surveying | Request quote | Learn process | Low |
| Trucking | Request freight quote | View fleet | Medium |
| Assisted living | Schedule tour | Download guide | Low (emotional) |
| Landscaping | Get estimate | View gallery | Medium |
| Tree service | Call for inspection | Emergency removal | High (storm) |
| Land clearing | Request quote | View before/after | Low |
| Towing | Call now | — | Extreme (stranded) |

Urgency level affects:
- Number of above-fold CTAs (more urgency = more phone prominence)
- Emergency band visibility
- Color temperature of CTA buttons (high urgency = red/orange, low = brand color)
