# Mission Control — Tier System

## Overview

Mission Control routes every build through one of four tiers. Tier is determined by the audit score, opportunity score, and operator's judgment.

Price ranges shown are suggested retail. Operator sets final pricing.

---

## Tier 1 — Premium AI Website Refresh

**Price:** $500 – $2,500  
**AI Cost:** ~$0.50

**Who it's for:**  
Any local service business with an outdated or missing website that needs a premium modern upgrade fast.

**What's included:**
- Premium hero section
- Service cards
- Trust strip
- Reviews/testimonials
- Quote form or call CTA
- Sticky mobile call button
- Local SEO structure
- Clean modern typography
- Subtle motion (reveal animations, hover, light drift)
- Mobile-first layout

**Visual standard:**  
Clean · Modern · Fast · Mobile-first · Light premium motion

**What it must have:**
- CTA visible above fold
- Phone number visible at all times
- Trust proof near top
- Industry-specific visual (not generic stock)

**What it must not have:**
- Heavy WebGL or 3D dependency
- Animation that delays comprehension
- Generic AI design patterns

---

## Tier 2 — Premium AI + Signature Motion

**Price:** $5,000 – $10,000  
**AI Cost:** ~$1.50 – $3

**Who it's for:**  
HVAC, roofing, surveying, trucking, contractors. Established businesses that need motion storytelling to stand out.

**What's included:**  
Everything in Tier 1, plus:
- Animated hero background
- Scroll reveals
- Hover card effects
- Industry-specific motion
- Stronger visual rhythm and storytelling

**Visual standard:**  
Animated hero · Scroll reveals · Hover effects · Motion storytelling

**What it must have:**
- Purposeful motion (airflow for HVAC, route lines for trucking, scan lines for survey)
- Mobile fallback for all motion
- CTA never hidden by animation

**What it must not have:**
- Animation spam
- Random stagger effects
- Motion that competes with the lead form

---

## Tier 3 — Premium AI + 3D Hero Experience

**Price:** $15,000 – $30,000  
**AI Cost:** ~$5 – $8

**Who it's for:**  
Established contractors, premium brands, companies needing major visual upgrade and stronger brand identity.

**What's included:**  
Everything in Tier 2, plus:
- Spline-style 3D hero centerpiece (or cinematic 3D render)
- Interactive or animated 3D object
- Mouse-follow effect
- Deeper brand identity
- Static fallback required

**Visual standard:**  
Spline-style 3D centerpiece · Interactive hero · Premium brand feel

**3D object examples by industry:**
- Roofing: 3D home/roof shield
- HVAC: 3D unit with airflow
- Survey: 3D scanner/tripod on topo map
- Trucking: 3D truck/fleet route scene
- Plumbing: 3D pipe/water flow
- Concrete: 3D slab/finish system
- Assisted living: 3D warm home/floor plan

**What it must not have:**
- Weak 3D decoration unrelated to business
- CTA hidden by visual
- 3D that breaks mobile performance

---

## Tier 4 — Premium Immersive Business Website

**Price:** $30,000+  
**AI Cost:** ~$10 – $15

**Who it's for:**  
Multi-location companies, high-ticket companies, serious-budget clients.

**What's included:**  
Everything in Tier 3, plus:
- Full WebGL / Three.js option
- Product / service configurator
- Interactive floor plan or fleet showcase
- CRM integration
- SEO automation
- Analytics / reporting
- Advanced conversion system

**Visual standard:**  
Full WebGL option · CRM · SEO · Automation · Analytics · Conversion system

**What it must have:**
- Mobile fallback for all WebGL
- Full performance testing before ship
- CRM / analytics properly wired
- Conversion tracking active

**What it must not have:**
- WebGL with no static fallback
- Slow mobile experience
- Motion over conversion
