# Mission Control — Sales Package Generator

The sales package is the outreach kit drafted after a build passes QA and the operator approves the preview.

No component of the sales package is sent without operator approval.

---

## Sales Package Components

Every sales package contains:

1. **Pitch Email** — cold outreach email to the business owner
2. **SMS** — short text message version (160 characters max)
3. **Call Script** — what to say when calling the business
4. **Proposal Summary** — one-page summary of what they're getting and why
5. **Before/After Framing** — visual or text comparison of current site vs. upgrade

---

## 1. Pitch Email

### Format

```
Subject: [Business Name] — Your website is costing you leads

Body:
[Opening: specific observation about their current site]
[Problem: what visitors experience when they visit]
[Solution: what the upgrade delivers]
[Proof: brief credibility signal]
[CTA: single clear ask]
[Signature]
```

### Rules

- Subject line must reference the specific business
- No generic "I came across your website" opening without specific detail
- Mention one specific problem from the audit (real, not invented)
- CTA is one ask only: reply, call, or view preview — not all three
- Maximum 200 words in the body
- No attachments in the first email
- No PDFs unless explicitly following up

### Good Subject Lines

- "[Business Name] — your roofing site is missing a mobile call button"
- "[Business Name] — 3 things pushing Cleveland homeowners away from your site"
- "Quick question about [Business Name]'s website"
- "[City] [industry] sites are getting more competitive — here's where you stand"

### Bad Subject Lines

- "Your website needs help"
- "I noticed something about your site"
- "Professional web design services"
- "Let me build you a website"

---

## 2. SMS

### Format

```
[Business Name] — quick note on your website. Found 3 issues that are costing you calls. Took 5 min to fix. Want to see? — [Your Name]
```

### Rules

- 160 characters max
- Must include business name for context
- Must reference a specific benefit (calls, leads, trust)
- Must have a clear question or ask at the end
- No links in the first SMS
- No pricing in the first SMS

---

## 3. Call Script

### Format

```
Opening:
"Hi, is this [Name]? My name is [Your Name]. I help local [industry] businesses in [city] upgrade their websites to get more calls and leads online. I took a look at [Business Name]'s site and found a few things that are probably pushing customers to your competitors. Do you have 2 minutes?"

If yes:
"Great. The main thing I noticed is [specific finding from audit]. That alone is likely costing you [estimated impact — calls, visibility, trust]. I put together a quick concept for a rebuild — could I send it to you?"

If no / busy:
"No problem. When's the best time to call back? I'll be quick."

Close:
"I'll send you a quick before/after of what I have in mind. Best email?"
```

### Rules

- Name the specific problem in the first 30 seconds
- Do not pitch features — pitch outcomes
- Do not quote price on the first call
- Keep under 2 minutes
- Always ask for permission to continue

---

## 4. Proposal Summary

### Format

One page, built around:

```
[Business Name] — Website Upgrade Proposal

The Problem:
[2–3 sentences summarizing audit findings in plain language]

The Solution:
[Tier X] — [Tier Name]
[3–4 bullet points of what's included]
[What the business will have after]

Investment:
$[price range] — [payment structure if applicable]

What Happens Next:
1. You approve the direction
2. We build it in [timeframe]
3. You review it before it goes live
4. We launch and you start getting more calls

Preview:
[link to preview]

Questions? [Contact info]
```

### Rules

- No feature lists longer than 5 items
- Price always shown as a range at first ("starting at $X")
- Timeline must be honest and achievable
- Preview link must be included if build passed QA
- No NDAs, proposals, or contracts in the first send unless specifically requested

---

## 5. Before/After Framing

Visual or text comparison:

```
BEFORE: [Client's current site]
- No mobile CTA
- Generic stock photo hero
- No reviews visible
- No service area
- No trust proof
- [Overall summary: "looks like a site from 2012"]

AFTER: [Mission Control build]
- Sticky mobile call button
- Premium industry-specific hero visual
- Trust strip with reviews and certifications
- Service area block
- Glass trust cards in hero
- [Overall summary: "looks like a brand worth $X/job"]
```

---

## Approval Workflow

```
Build passes QA
  → Sales package drafted automatically
  → Operator views all 5 components in Mission Control
  → Operator can edit any component
  → Operator approves each channel individually:
      [ ] Approve Email
      [ ] Approve SMS
      [ ] Approve Call Script
      [ ] Approve Proposal Summary
  → Approved components sent to operator's preferred outreach tool
  → Operator sends manually or via connected CRM
```

No send without per-channel approval.
Mission Control drafts. The operator sends.
